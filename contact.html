Before we
get started.

Some useful details to prepare before sending a fabrication enquiry.

What information should I share for a quotation?

Share drawings if available, approximate dimensions, material or grade requirements, quantity, finish, delivery location and target schedule.

Can you work from client-provided drawings?

Yes. The enquiry can begin with client drawings and specifications. The scope and any clarifications should be agreed before fabrication starts.

Which types of steelwork do you support?

Typical scopes include structural frames, beams, columns, trusses, industrial shed components, platforms, stairs, handrails and custom welded assemblies.

Do you support repairs or modifications?

Repair, reinforcement and modification enquiries can be reviewed based on site conditions, access, drawings and the condition of the existing structure.

How soon will someone respond?

Response time depends on the enquiry details and business availability. Include your preferred contact method and project timeline so the team can prioritize appropriately.
